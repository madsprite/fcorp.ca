A Letter from The Founders

To those who share in our future,

This was another year of quiet progress. Our customers grew more comfortable, and comfort, as you know, is the most durable form of loyalty. Very few of them noticed anything had changed. We consider this our finest achievement.

We continued to restructure ourselves around the people we serve. Forty thousand roles were released into opportunity. The customers they served were reassured that nothing would change, and nothing did.

Our guidance for the coming year is the same as it has always been: continuity. They will stay because they have always stayed. We will be here because we have always been here.

With gratitude, as always,

The Founders

  3. Switching cost includes inconvenience, uncertainty, and the feeling of leaving a friend. See note 4.
